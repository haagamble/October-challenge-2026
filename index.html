    How it works / FAQ

    Choose 3 activities on scoring days and enter what you actually did: reps, minutes, seconds, stairs, or self-assessed points for Other.

    Break activities into whatever sets work for you. Your daily total counts, even if you spread it throughout the day.

    Each activity counts up to 100 points. If it is today's double-points activity, it can count up to 200. The daily max is 400.

    100-point targets Push ups: 50 reps Pull ups: 10 full or 50 modified reps Squats/Lunges: 100 reps Sit ups: 100 reps Plank: 5 minutes Running/Walking: 40 minutes Strength: 100 reps Dancing/Aerobics: 25 minutes Stairs: 400 stairs Bird dog: 50 right-left pairs Other: a doable but challenging amount of exercise for you

    Other covers swimming, football, cycling, and other unlisted activities. Award yourself up to 100 points total each day across these activities, with 100 meaning doable but challenging for you. Use a consistent standard, and only count exercise you have not logged in another category. Other counts as one of your 3 choices and does not receive double points.

    What does the daily cheerleader do?

    One participant per Pacific-time day is invited to encourage our WhatsApp group, share updates, and remind people to log activities or take Rest. The rotation includes everyone who joined by the end of October 1, Pacific time.

    If you are assigned, accept the role or pass. Passing does not reassign it. After encouraging the group, check the completion box to claim 100 bonus points; you may uncheck an accidental claim that same day. This is self-reported and optional. You can reconsider a pass during the day. Only the assigned participant can claim, once per date; refreshing cannot add extra bonuses.

    The bonus adds to personal and team totals, but does not increase the goal, use an activity choice, or receive double points. It is available on Rest days. Activity points alone determine participation starts, rest eligibility, daily activity averages, and the 400 Club. You can earn 400 activity points plus a 100-point bonus. Daily history labels bonuses separately.

    Full or modified pull ups?

    Select Pull ups, then choose Full or Modified in its pull-up type menu. Full pull ups earn 10 points per rep (100 points at 10 reps). Modified pull ups earn 2 points per rep (100 points at 50 reps). Both count as one activity choice, with a shared 100-point base cap and up to 200 points on pull-up double-points days.

    Your last choice is remembered on this browser for future days. Each day saves its own type, so changing today's choice recalculates today's points without changing earlier days. Older entries without a type count as Full.

    What counts in the combined activities and Strength?

    Running/Walking: Add your running and walking minutes together. Every 2 completed minutes earns 5 points, up to 100 base points at 40 minutes. An extra odd minute earns no additional points until the next minute is completed. This is one activity choice, with one shared daily cap.

    Squats/Lunges: Add your squat and lunge repetitions together. Each squat or lunge earns 1 point, up to 100 base points. One lunge on one leg counts as one rep; a left and right lunge count as two. This is one activity choice.

    Strength: Exercises using weights, resistance bands, or your own body weight. Examples include curls, presses, rows, kettlebell swings, chair dips, glute bridges, and calf raises. Add your repetitions together: 1 rep earns 1 point, up to 100 base points. Count each completed repetition, not the weight lifted. Count each exercise in only one category. Strength counts as one of your three activity choices and can earn double points on its scheduled days.

    Dancing/Aerobics: Count time spent dancing or doing aerobic workouts, including following an aerobics video. Add those minutes together: 4 points per minute, up to 100 base points. This is one activity choice with one shared cap.

    Count each exercise only once. For example, log weighted squats under Squats/Lunges or Strength, not both. Do not also count the same exercise under Other.

    How do rest days work?

    Each full challenge week has six scoring days and one required rest day. The weeks are October 1-7, 8-14, 15-21, and 22-28. There are no rest days for October 29-31. Everyone chooses their own day; you do not need to rest together.

    Select Rest today on the day you want to rest. You cannot choose a future date, but you can preview this week's double-points activities to plan ahead. Rest earns zero points and activity logging is disabled for that day. You can still exercise, but it will not earn challenge points. A Rest selection lasts for the day. If you already logged points, clear today's activity values before choosing Rest.

    If you have not used your rest day, the first completed day with zero points automatically becomes Rest, after midnight Pacific time. Today stays open for evening logging. If you score on the first six days, the seventh automatically becomes Rest. Further missed days do not become extra rest days, and allowances do not carry over. Days participated includes today: positive activity points or a valid rest day count as participation. A cheerleader bonus alone does not count.

    When am I eligible, and how does Rest affect the goal?

    Participation starts with your first positive points, not simply joining. You receive a rest day only if you started by the opening day of that week. For example, first scoring on October 3 means no rest allowance for October 3-7; your next allowance begins October 8.

    You may also start by selecting Rest on October 1, 8, 15, or 22. This uses that week's rest day and begins your participation without requiring points first. Merely joining and missing days does not start participation or assign Rest.

    The goal is 250 points per scoring day from your start through October 31. Each eligible full week contributes 1,500 points, with its rest allowance built in immediately. Starting October 1 contributes 6,750 points; starting October 3 contributes 6,500. Additional missed days still count toward the goal. Daily team pace excludes assigned rest days; until you use an allowance, it is reserved for that week's final day. People can see Rest status and team totals, but not each person's daily score.

    Challenge days follow Pacific time. The 400 club celebrates people who reached the daily max yesterday.

    Push ups may be wall pushups, knee pushups, or full pushups. Sit ups may be full sit ups or crunches. Stairs can be up and/or down. One bird dog rep means a right-left pair. Dancing may be ballet, breakdancing, kitchen dancing, or any movement you are prepared to defend as rhythmic.
